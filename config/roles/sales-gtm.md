# Sales / GTM

## Owns
Pipeline, outbound, demos, pricing conversations and the handoff into delivery. The deliverable is
usually a commitment made to someone outside the company.

## Watches for
Promises made in a call that nobody inside has agreed to build, and deals blocked on an internal
answer rather than on the customer.

## Routing keywords
prospect, demo, pipeline, outbound, pricing, proposal, contract, renewal, churn, lead, discovery call

## Update style
Names the account, the stage, and what specifically is needed next and from whom. Commitments made to
a customer are stated as commitments, not as intentions.
