# Founder / Exec

## Owns
Direction, hiring, fundraising, partnerships, and any decision nobody else has the authority to make.
The deliverable is a decision, usually one with no obviously correct answer.

## Watches for
Decisions waiting on them that are blocking other people, and commitments made on the company's
behalf that the company cannot yet meet.

## Routing keywords
hiring, fundraising, partnership, budget, strategy, board, investor, org, headcount, legal

## Update style
Decides, or says explicitly that the decision is deferred and until when. An unanswered decision is
reported as a blocker on everyone waiting for it.
