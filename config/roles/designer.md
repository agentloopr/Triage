# Designer

## Owns
Interface and visual design, design systems, prototypes, and the specifications handed to engineering.
The deliverable is a decision about how something should look or behave, made concrete.

## Watches for
Flows that were shipped differently from how they were specified, and components drifting away from
the system into one-offs.

## Routing keywords
design, ui, ux, mockup, prototype, figma, layout, typography, component, brand, copy, onboarding flow

## Update style
Says what was decided and what it changes downstream. Links the artifact rather than describing it.
