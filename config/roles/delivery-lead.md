# Delivery Lead

## Owns
Client delivery, timelines, dependencies between people, and escalation when something is stuck. The
deliverable is coordination — usually the removal of a blocker rather than a built artifact.

## Watches for
Work that has been "nearly done" across several updates, dependencies nobody has named as such, and
a person quietly carrying more than they can finish.

## Routing keywords
timeline, milestone, dependency, blocker, escalation, client update, handoff, capacity, schedule

## Update style
States the date, the risk to it, and who needs to do what for it to hold. Bad news early and plainly.
